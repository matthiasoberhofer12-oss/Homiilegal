Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Homii app ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address (required)
  • Name / Display Name
  • Authentication credentials via Firebase Authentication

1.2 Login Methods

You can sign in using:

  • Email and password
  • Google Login (via Firebase Authentication)
  • Apple Login (via Firebase Authentication)

For Google and Apple Login, Firebase securely handles authentication, and we receive your email and name as provided by the service.

1.3 App Data

We collect data related to your use of the app:

  • WG membership (wgId)
  • Tasks (title, description, due date)
  • Expenses (amount, title, category)
  • Receipt photos (Premium feature, stored in Firebase Storage)
  • Votes / Voting data
  • Shopping list items
  • Notifications (in-app notifications, e.g., task assignments)
  • Leaderboard data (points, streaks, XP)

1.4 Data Not Collected

We currently do NOT collect:

  • Comments or chat messages
  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Calendar data
  • Other sensitive documents (except receipts)

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Operate and improve the Homii app
  • Manage WG tasks, expenses, and voting
  • Provide in-app notifications
  • Display leaderboard and app statistics

3. Where We Store Your Data

All data is stored securely using Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Firestore: Users, WGs, tasks, expenses, votings, shopping lists, notifications
  • Firebase Storage: Profile pictures and receipt photos
  • Firebase Authentication: Email/password and OAuth credentials

4. Analytics & Tracking

We currently do not use analytics, tracking, or third-party advertising services. Crash reporting is prepared but not active. Apple Tracking Transparency (ATT) is not applicable.

5. Sharing Data

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties, except as required for Firebase services (Google) to provide authentication and storage.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Withdraw consent for data collection where applicable
  • Contact us regarding privacy concerns
